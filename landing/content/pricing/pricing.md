faqTitle: Questions, answered.
faqs:
  - question: Is the open-source version really free?
    answer: Yes. WuppyCMS is MIT licensed. Run it on your own server, keep your content in Git and pay nothing to us. Cloud is for teams that want us to run it.
  - question: Can I switch plans later?
    answer: Yes. Upgrade, downgrade or cancel from your dashboard. When you move up, the new limits apply immediately; when you move down, they apply at the next billing period.
  - question: What happens to my content if I leave?
    answer: Your content is Markdown and MDX in a Git repository. Export it or point WuppyCMS at the same repository on your own server. There is no proprietary database to migrate.
  - question: Do I need a credit card to start?
    answer: You can create an account and start with one site without a sales call. Paid plans are billed monthly or yearly, and you can cancel any time.
  - question: How do custom domains work?
    answer: Point an A record at the server and add the domain in your project. WuppyCMS issues and renews the SSL certificate automatically.
  - question: Does WuppyCMS Cloud include MCP?
    answer: Yes. Every cloud plan includes the MCP endpoint and API tokens, so Claude Code, Cursor, Codex and opencode can read and update content with your permissions.
