## Dashboard

The dashboard is the first screen after sign-in. It shows only what your role may see.

- **Drafts**, **Media files** and **Users** cards with counts.
- **Content**: each collection with its published and draft counts. Click a count to open the filtered list.
- **Recent activity**: the latest changed entries with status, collection and time.
- Quick actions: **+ Page**, **+ Post** and **Upload media**.

A new site shows a short guide to create the first page or post instead.

## Calendar

**Calendar** places entries on a month or week view by their `publishAt` or `date` field. Colours show the status:
draft, scheduled or published.

- Drag an entry to another day to change its date (needs the scheduling permission; the change is a commit).
- Click a day to see its entries, edit one quickly or create a new one on that date.
- Dates follow the site's time zone and your language.
