Visual editor: theme Astro components show their real output on the canvas (`/_wuppy/component/<Name>`, generated by the integration, rendered without props). A `.md` entry that is not valid MDX yet and gets a component now reports the MDX error instead of "Disallowed content: <Component>". A component that throws without the props it needs (e.g. a table of contents without `headings`) no longer breaks the page: it renders as an HTML comment, the error is logged and the canvas says it needs props.
